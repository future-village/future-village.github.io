# Art credits / 美術出處

site/assets/art/ 的圖像由本專案使用 AI 生圖工具製作，經人工挑選與處理，不含第三方角色。
Images in site/assets/art/ were created for this project with AI image generation tools, then selected and processed by humans. They contain no third-party characters.

Project-created artwork uses CC BY 4.0; see [LICENSE-CONTENT.md](LICENSE-CONTENT.md) for scope and attribution. Household contributions retain their separate terms, source and AI attribution in their JSON records.

## 公共道具 / Shared props

只收 CC0。新增或更換 `site/world/assets/models/` 直屬的 `.glb` 時，同一筆變更在下面加一行，且全檔只能有這一行寫到那個檔名。該行要同時有檔名、`CC0`、`https://` 來源、授權檔名 `LICENSE*.txt`。授權檔放在模型目錄直屬，正文也要是 CC0。住戶自己的房子圖不寫進這份清單。生產房子外觀仍是 `site/assets/art/house_1.png` 到 `house_6.png`。

樹、松、路燈、長椅、花叢，以及模型尚未換掉的圍籬卡，仍是 `site/assets/art/props/` 的手繪卡，沿用上面的站方美術。

- fence_run.glb — CC0. Kenney Fantasy Town Kit 2.0. Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/fantasy-town-kit . License file: LICENSE-kenney-fantasy-town-kit.txt

Shared props are CC0 only. A new or replaced `.glb` directly under `site/world/assets/models/` needs exactly one line below in the same change. That line carries the file name, `CC0`, an `https://` source, and a `LICENSE*.txt` name. The license file sits directly in the models directory and its text is CC0. Household house pictures stay off this list. Shared house cards remain `site/assets/art/house_1.png` through `house_6.png`.

Trees, pines, lamps, benches, flower bushes, and the fence card still shown as a picture remain hand-drawn files under `site/assets/art/props/`, under the project-art terms above.
